Duty-Free Imports in Kenya

Who qualifies and the required documents to process your duty-free vehicle import.

Diplomats, returning residents under certain schemes, and other eligible importers may qualify for duty exemptions in Kenya. You will need valid identification, proof of eligibility, assignment letters where applicable, and KRA documentation. Speedgate Logistics guides you through each step so your duty-free import is processed correctly the first time.

Japan vs UK Imports

Choosing the right source market for your budget and vehicle preference in Kenya.

Japan offers wide used-car stock, competitive auction prices, and popular models like Toyota and Subaru. The UK suits buyers seeking premium brands, diesel options, and specific trims. Compare total landed cost—not just purchase price—including freight, insurance, duty, and clearance before you decide.

KRA Duty Breakdown

Understanding all the landed cost line items and how duties are calculated in Kenya.

Your landed cost typically includes CIF value, import duty, excise duty, VAT, IDF/RDL fees, port charges, and clearance agent fees. KRA uses the vehicle age, engine size, and depreciated value to assess duty. We provide a clear quotation upfront so there are no surprises at Mombasa port.
